# Teaching Notes & User Preferences

- **Learner Focus**: Wants deep, rigorous understanding of System One models (Jev AI and Laya), not just high-level marketing summaries.
- **Hardware Capability**: Local RTX 4090 GPU (24GB VRAM) available on Ubuntu Linux.
- **Platform & Playground Requirement**: Build an interactive evaluation platform/playground comparing Generative Model Tool Calling vs System One Executable Decisions.
- **Aesthetic Direction**: Must follow [Beautiful UI](https://www.beautifului.dev/) primitives:
  - Dark sleek AI-native look
  - Micro-shimmer / pixel-grid loaders
  - Thinking and reasoning traces
  - Tool chips with latency & token metrics
  - Recommendation cards with calibrated confidence gauges
  - Approval cards for human-in-the-loop branching
- **Pedagogical Strategy (Teach Skill)**:
  - Tufte / clean layout for lessons and reference materials.
  - Distinct separation of Fluency Strength vs Storage Strength (using retrieval practice & desirable difficulty).
  - Tightly coupled feedback loops in lessons.
